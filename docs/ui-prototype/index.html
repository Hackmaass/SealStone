This agreement is between the Campus Incubation Cell (the funder) and the KrishiLens team (the builders). Dr. Asha Mehta, faculty mentor, will settle any disagreement between them. The Cell commits a total grant of 5 ETH (test network) for the work below.

Milestone 1: problem validation

The team submits a report on interviews with at least 15 farmers about crop disease losses, due 31 October 2026. On approval the Cell pays 1 ETH.

Milestone 2: working prototype

The team delivers an app that identifies crop diseases from a phone photo. The second payment of 2.5 ETH is released when the prototype works to the mentor's satisfaction1, due 30 November 2026. If the Cell does not reply within a week, the payment is considered approved.2

Milestone 3: field pilot

The team runs a pilot with 20 farmers and shares a short results report. The Cell pays the remaining funds after the first two payments3 once the pilot is done4. The team earns an extra 0.5 ETH if the pilot finishes before 30 November.5
